# Project Brief

## 1. Project Overview

### 1.1 Working Project Name
**Luxury Hotel Website Demo**

### 1.2 Initial Prospect Context
The first concept is tailored to **Kandy Victoria Eco Resort** as a premium hospitality sales demo. The system must remain reusable for future hotels, resorts, villas, and boutique stays without becoming a generic template.

### 1.3 Primary Goal
Create a polished, production-quality hospitality website demo that makes a hotel owner immediately understand the business value of a premium website and credibly supports an initial commercial quote of approximately **LKR 120,000**.

## 2. Business Objective

### 2.1 What We Are Selling
The offer is a custom hospitality web experience designed to improve brand perception, direct enquiry and booking intent, mobile conversion, presentation of rooms and experiences, independence from third-party channels, and long-term discoverability.

### 2.2 Commercial Positioning
The website sits above low-cost template and brochure-site offerings. Visible design, content, interaction, speed, trust, and technical quality must communicate premium value.

### 2.3 Initial Pricing Anchor
The initial commercial anchor is **LKR 120,000**. If a client needs a lower price, reduce scope rather than discounting the complete premium package without changing deliverables.

## 3. Target Users

### 3.1 Guest Segments
Primary audiences include international leisure travellers, Sri Lankan domestic travellers, couples, small groups, families where room inventory supports them, and mobile users arriving from Google, Maps, social media, or booking platforms.

### 3.2 Client-Side User
The hotel owner or manager must be able to understand how the site improves property presentation, trust, enquiry flow, and direct-booking potential within the first minute of the demo.

## 4. Experience Principles

### 4.1 Quiet Luxury
The experience should feel calm, spacious, confident, warm, and premium rather than flashy.

### 4.2 Destination First
Sell the feeling of the stay and the destination before presenting a dense facilities list.

### 4.3 Photography-Led Storytelling
Use high-quality property and destination imagery for emotional impact. Keep copy concise and purposeful.

### 4.4 Conversion Without Pressure
Booking and enquiry actions should remain available throughout the journey without making the site feel like aggressive e-commerce.

### 4.5 Mobile as a First-Class Experience
Navigation, booking actions, gallery behaviour, typography, media, and performance must be designed specifically for small screens.

## 5. Initial Scope

### 5.1 Core Pages
Home, Stay, individual Room/Suite pages, Experiences, Dining, Gallery, Kandy/Location, Offers, Contact, FAQ, and an Availability/Enquiry flow.

### 5.2 Core Conversion Actions
Check Availability, Book/Enquire, WhatsApp, Call, and Contact.

### 5.3 Trust Elements
Verified ratings, attributed reviews, accurate room facts, location context, amenity clarity, policies, and trustworthy contact details.

## 6. Visual Direction

### 6.1 Working Mood
**Quiet Luxury / Tropical Hill Country / Nature-Led Boutique Retreat**

### 6.2 Palette Direction
Use deep forest-charcoal, warm ivory, muted bronze, lake-slate, and warm stone neutrals. Avoid bright cliché eco-green styling.

### 6.3 Typography Direction
Use an elegant editorial serif for display typography paired with a highly readable modern sans-serif for body and interface text.

## 7. Quality Bar

### 7.1 Design
The demo should show compositional discipline comparable with leading international luxury-hospitality websites while remaining original.

### 7.2 Engineering
Use reusable components, responsive behaviour, accessible interactions, optimised media, SEO-ready markup, analytics hooks, maintainable code, and deployment readiness.

### 7.3 Performance
Large photography and cinematic media must not become an excuse for a slow site. Use responsive media, modern formats, lazy loading, mobile-specific asset decisions, and reduced-motion fallbacks.

## 8. Demo Rules

### 8.1 Verified Information
Property-specific claims must be verified before public publication. Unverified ideas may appear only as clearly labelled internal concept content.

### 8.2 Photography and Copyright
Final production must use client-owned or properly licensed media. Third-party images are research references only unless permission exists.

### 8.3 Inspiration Versus Copying
Reference sites may inspire principles such as spacing, storytelling, booking visibility, and information hierarchy, but layouts, copy, branding, and proprietary assets must not be cloned.

## 9. Success Criteria

### 9.1 Commercial Success Criteria
The demo succeeds when a hotel owner can quickly understand the value proposition, the experience looks intentionally premium rather than template-based, and the quality can credibly support the LKR 120,000 anchor.

### 9.2 Product Success Criteria
Mobile and desktop both feel complete; rooms and destination content are easy to explore; enquiry actions are clear; the architecture is reusable; and factual content remains trustworthy.

## 10. Next Step

### 10.1 Documentation Sequence
Proceed through the documentation roadmap before implementation so Codex receives a stable design, UX, content, and technical specification rather than a collection of ad-hoc prompts.
