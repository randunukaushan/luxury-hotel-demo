# Client Handoff and Commercial Package

## 1. Document Purpose

### 1.1 Objective
Define how the premium hotel website is presented, sold, delivered, and handed over around an initial **LKR 120,000** commercial anchor.

### 1.2 Commercial Principle
The client is buying a custom hospitality sales and brand experience, not a cheap template installation.

## 2. Package Name

### 2.1 Working Name
**Premium Hotel Website Package**

### 2.2 Starting Price
**LKR 120,000**

This is the starting quote for the documented base scope. Final price may change if scope changes.

## 3. Core Client Value

### 3.1 Brand Value
Create a property-owned premium digital identity rather than depending only on OTA/listing pages.

### 3.2 Conversion Value
Give guests direct availability, WhatsApp, phone, and enquiry paths.

### 3.3 Decision Value
Present room facts, dining, location, gallery, FAQ, and guest proof clearly.

### 3.4 Long-Term Value
Create a reusable technical foundation for offers, new rooms, booking-engine integration, and future marketing.

## 4. Base Package Deliverables

### 4.1 Design
Includes:

- custom premium visual direction;
- responsive desktop/tablet/mobile design;
- hospitality-specific layout system;
- custom page composition;
- reusable component system.

### 4.2 Pages
Base package includes the documented core set:

- Home;
- Stay;
- Room detail templates;
- Experiences;
- Dining;
- Gallery;
- Kandy/Location;
- Offers;
- FAQ;
- Contact;
- Availability/Enquiry.

### 4.3 Conversion
Includes:

- Check Availability flow;
- WhatsApp;
- click-to-call;
- contact form;
- mobile action bar.

### 4.4 CMS
Includes structured content management for the agreed public content types.

### 4.5 SEO Foundation
Includes technical on-page foundation:

- metadata support;
- sitemap;
- robots;
- canonical setup;
- structured data where appropriate;
- semantic page structure.

### 4.6 Performance and Accessibility
Includes responsive image handling, reduced-motion support, keyboard-accessible major interactions, and the documented quality pass.

### 4.7 Analytics
Includes basic conversion event setup if the client supplies/approves the analytics account.

## 5. Client Responsibilities

### 5.1 Content Accuracy
Client confirms:

- official room inventory;
- amenities;
- policies;
- contact information;
- offers;
- dining information;
- experience availability.

### 5.2 Media
Client supplies owned/licensed photography unless photography is separately quoted.

### 5.3 Accounts
Client supplies or approves accounts for:

- domain;
- commercial hosting;
- CMS;
- transactional email;
- analytics;
- booking engine if applicable.

## 6. Exclusions From Base Price

### 6.1 Not Included Unless Quoted
The LKR 120,000 base package does not automatically include:

- professional photography;
- videography;
- custom logo/brand identity creation;
- custom payment gateway;
- PMS/channel manager;
- real-time inventory engine;
- booking-engine subscription;
- multilingual translation;
- copywriting in large volume;
- paid stock/licensing fees;
- premium third-party subscriptions;
- domain registration/renewal;
- hosting subscription;
- large post-launch content entry;
- custom CRM;
- email marketing automation;
- ongoing SEO campaign;
- ongoing social media management.

## 7. Third-Party Costs

### 7.1 Separation Rule
Third-party recurring charges should be paid directly by the client whenever practical.

### 7.2 Typical Categories
Potential costs:

- domain;
- Vercel Pro or another commercial host;
- paid CMS plan if needed;
- booking engine;
- email volume beyond included tiers;
- analytics or consent platform if paid.

### 7.3 No Hidden Markup
If a third-party charge is passed through, identify it separately from the website build fee.

## 8. Payment Structure

### 8.1 Recommended Milestones
Default commercial structure:

- **50% deposit — LKR 60,000** to start production;
- **50% balance — LKR 60,000** before production launch/transfer.

### 8.2 Alternative Milestones
For larger custom scope, use staged payments such as deposit, staging approval, and launch.

### 8.3 Deposit Principle
Do not begin full client-specific production work without an agreed deposit unless intentionally doing a limited internal sales concept.

## 9. Negotiation Strategy

### 9.1 Price Objection
If the client cannot afford LKR 120,000, reduce deliverables rather than silently delivering the full premium package at a large discount.

### 9.2 Scope Reduction Examples
Possible reductions:

- fewer custom pages;
- no CMS for selected content;
- simplified gallery;
- no offers module;
- simpler experience section;
- fewer room migrations;
- no advanced analytics;
- reduced content-entry work.

### 9.3 Quality Boundary
Do not reduce core security, basic accessibility, responsive quality, or truthful content standards to lower price.

## 10. Proposal Presentation

### 10.1 Lead With Outcome
Present:

**Stronger property brand + easier direct enquiry + better mobile guest experience**

before discussing frameworks or technical implementation.

### 10.2 Demo Sequence
Show:

1. premium home first impression;
2. room comparison;
3. mobile experience;
4. direct availability/WhatsApp;
5. gallery and Kandy storytelling;
6. CMS editing;
7. quality/performance approach.

### 10.3 Price Reveal
Present LKR 120,000 after the client understands the deliverables and business outcome.

## 11. Change Requests

### 11.1 Included Revisions
The final proposal should specify a limited number of reasonable design/content revision rounds.

### 11.2 Scope Change
New page types, new integrations, structural redesign, or major features after approval require a revised quote.

### 11.3 Written Approval
Keep important scope changes documented in writing.

## 12. Content Approval

### 12.1 Pre-Launch Sign-Off
Client must confirm factual property content before production launch.

### 12.2 Ratings and Offers
Client acknowledges that ratings, prices, offers, and policies can become stale and require maintenance.

## 13. Handoff

### 13.1 Ownership Package
At completion, hand over:

- production website;
- repository/project access as agreed;
- hosting access/ownership;
- CMS access;
- domain/DNS configuration notes;
- analytics access;
- environment-variable inventory without exposing secrets in documents;
- content editing instructions;
- deployment/maintenance notes.

### 13.2 Credentials
Never send passwords or secret API keys inside the Git repository.

## 14. Training

### 14.1 CMS Walkthrough
Provide a short walkthrough covering:

- editing room content;
- adding/removing offers;
- changing gallery images;
- updating FAQ;
- updating contact information;
- publishing content safely.

### 14.2 Recording
A short screen recording may be provided if included in the final client agreement.

## 15. Post-Launch Support

### 15.1 Defect Window
Recommended base package includes a **14-day defect support window** for issues caused by the delivered implementation.

### 15.2 Not Included
The defect window does not mean unlimited:

- redesign;
- new features;
- content entry;
- third-party platform changes;
- marketing work.

### 15.3 Maintenance
Offer ongoing maintenance separately if the client wants updates, monitoring, content changes, or analytics reporting.

## 16. Production Ownership

### 16.1 Client-Controlled Accounts
Prefer client ownership for domain, hosting, CMS, and paid third-party services.

### 16.2 Developer Access
Developer receives only the access required to build and maintain the site.

### 16.3 Exit Safety
The client should not be trapped because the developer personally owns the domain or essential business account.

## 17. Intellectual Property and Assets

### 17.1 Client Assets
Client remains responsible for rights to supplied logos, photos, copy, and media.

### 17.2 Third-Party Libraries
Open-source and third-party software remain governed by their licenses.

### 17.3 Custom Work
Commercial agreement should clearly state ownership/licensing of the final custom implementation after full payment.

## 18. Privacy and Guest Data

### 18.1 Enquiries
Collect only information needed to respond to the guest.

### 18.2 Analytics
Do not send enquiry PII to analytics.

### 18.3 Retention
If the client later stores enquiries in a CRM/database, define retention and access rules separately.

## 19. Launch Checklist With Client

### 19.1 Approval
Confirm:

- pages;
- content;
- contact details;
- room facts;
- forms;
- WhatsApp;
- phone;
- policies;
- legal/privacy text;
- analytics consent;
- domain.

### 19.2 Billing
Confirm:

- final payment;
- recurring services transferred/owned;
- optional maintenance agreement.

## 20. Sales Positioning Statement

### 20.1 Internal Working Statement
**We build a custom premium hotel website that turns third-party discovery into an owned brand experience with clear rooms, destination storytelling, and direct guest enquiry paths.**

### 20.2 What We Do Not Sell
Do not present the service as “just a website with some pages.”

## 21. LKR 120,000 Justification

### 21.1 Included Work
The price is supported by the combined work across:

- research;
- property positioning;
- custom design system;
- responsive UX;
- multiple hospitality page types;
- CMS;
- enquiry conversion;
- SEO foundation;
- performance optimisation;
- accessibility;
- analytics;
- deployment;
- client handoff.

### 21.2 Demonstration Principle
The demo must make this value visible before the client sees a detailed feature list.

## 22. Final Commercial Rule

### 22.1 Scope for Price
If price changes, scope changes deliberately.

### 22.2 Trust
Do not make unsupported revenue or booking-growth promises.

### 22.3 Outcome
Sell a stronger owned digital channel and a better guest decision experience, not guaranteed financial results.
