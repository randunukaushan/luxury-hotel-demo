# Stock Image Credits

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Demo Use
The current website uses selected Pexels photography only to demonstrate the intended premium visual direction of the private sales concept.

### 1.2 Property Representation
These stock photographs are **not photographs of Kandy Victoria Eco Resort** unless the property later supplies or explicitly approves a specific asset.

The final public production website should replace room, property, dining, service, and other property-specific concept photography with client-owned or otherwise approved media.

## 2. Licensing Boundary

### 2.1 Source
The selected concept photographs are sourced from Pexels.

### 2.2 License Review
At the time of selection on 19 September 2026, Pexels stated that its photos and videos may be used free of charge under the Pexels license, including on websites and for commercial purposes.

### 2.3 Endorsement
The use of a stock photograph must not imply that:

- the photographer endorses the hotel;
- a depicted property is Kandy Victoria Eco Resort;
- a depicted room is an actual room category at Kandy Victoria Eco Resort;
- a depicted meal is currently served by the property.

## 4. Production Replacement Plan

### 4.1 Priority Replacements
Before public client launch, request and replace concept photography in this order:

1. hero/property landscape;
2. each official room category;
3. bathrooms and balconies;
4. restaurant and real dishes;
5. breakfast;
6. exterior and common areas;
7. guest/service moments;
8. property-specific experiences.

### 4.2 Content Verification
When replacing an image, verify that captions, room names, amenities, views, dining claims, and experience descriptions still match the actual property.

## 5. Technical Use

### 5.1 Remote Image Delivery
The private concept currently loads selected Pexels assets through Next.js Image optimisation and the Pexels image CDN.

### 5.2 Production Media
For production, prefer the client CMS/image pipeline so the website does not depend on stock-photo URLs for core property presentation.
